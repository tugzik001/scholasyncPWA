Built for real classrooms.

Slow data, shared phones and school computers. ScholaSync is designed around all three.

Fully offline

Pseudonymous Student ID

Students get a short ID like AAAA0001UNN. No email or phone number is collected from the student.

Teachers mark Monday to Friday in seconds. Anyone missing 3 or more classes turns red automatically.
